Making picks & winning

Pick every game

Select one winner for every regular-season matchup. Picks may be updated until that individual game begins.

Games lock at kickoff

Each game locks separately at its scheduled kickoff. Later games remain open even after earlier games have started.

✓

Most correct wins

The player with the most correct selections is the weekly champion. Weekly prize money is not paid separately.

×

Missed picks lose

Any game without a submitted selection at kickoff is automatically recorded as an incorrect pick.

The Monday Night tiebreaker

Each player predicts the combined final score of the week’s designated Monday Night game. Among players tied for the most correct picks, the prediction closest to the actual combined score wins.

03

The season championship

How overall rank is determined

Winning weeks matters most

The season score combines weekly victories with full-season picking accuracy. Both categories are normalized against the leading player so the calculation stays fair as the season develops.

60% Weekly Wins40% Correct Picks
Season Score= 60 × (your weekly wins ÷ most weekly wins) + 40 × (your correct picks ÷ most correct picks)

How it works: Your Season Score combines weekly wins and total correct picks. Weekly wins make up 60% of the score, and correct picks make up 40%. In each category, your score is based on how you compare with the current leader. The highest possible Season Score is 100 points.

04

Technical issues with play

Requesting assistance

!

Assistance is available when a genuine need arises

If a site, device, or connectivity issue prevents a player from submitting or updating picks, the Site Administrator may assist with the requested change, provided the conditions below are met.

  • The request must be sent by text or email before the applicable game kicks off.
  • The request must clearly identify the specific pick or change being requested.
  • One request may include multiple games that have not yet started.
  • No pick may be added or changed after that game has begun.
  • Assistance is intended for genuine site, device, access, or connectivity issues that prevent normal submission.
  • The request time, entry time, player, requested change, and Administrator action will be recorded.
SG

Fair play by design

An independent Administrator

The Sonoran Games Administrator manages the pool but does not participate as a player. The Administrator makes no picks, does not appear in rankings, cannot earn weekly victories, and is never eligible for prize money.
