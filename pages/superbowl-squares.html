How Super Bowl Squares Works
  • Click an empty square to claim it.
  • Enter your initials (and optional name).
  • Your claim is saved to a Google Sheet so everyone sees the same board.
  • If you don’t see your claim right away, hit “Refresh Board”.

Numbers (0–9) can be added later if you want. This version focuses on claiming squares.
